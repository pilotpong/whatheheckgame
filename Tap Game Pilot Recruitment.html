Two strands climb the screen, one solid, one dashed. Every point is drawn at once, all identical, and nothing marks the live one.
Climb one point at a time, alternating strands. The HUD says which strand is live; which point is yours to remember. A hit flashes and fades.
The beep is the only signal, and it comes at an unpredictable moment. Tap the point you believe is next, as fast as you can after it.
Tapping early, inside 100 ms, on the wrong point, on no point, or not at all is an error, and the same point fires again. Miss twice in a row and it is shown to you.
The first two boards keep the strands apart. After that they cross and weave.
